01

Create

Choose two HTTPS pages and a token to watch. A watch opens only if both pages currently agree on whether that token is present. Your GEN bond remains locked until a split is found or the deadline expires.

02

Hunt

Anyone can inspect an open watch. The funder and finder do not need to be the same wallet. The bounty board surfaces recent on-chain watches without changing the contract.

03

Claim

Before the deadline, Recheck asks the contract to read both sources again. If their token-presence results differ, the watch becomes SPLIT and the caller is recorded as finder. 95% of the bond is paid to the finder and 5% to the protocol fee recipient.

04

Refund

If an OPEN watch reaches its deadline without a successful split, Refund returns the full bond to the original funder.

05

Limits

SplitSignal currently checks token presence in rendered HTML, not a structured numeric value. URLs must be HTTPS. Watch windows are 1–168 hours. The minimum bond is 0.01 GEN.
