What the contract does

Validators render both pages and agree with strict_eq. The app does not score them.

SPLIT pays the caller once. A second recheck reverts. AGREED does not move the bond. An unreadable page does not pay. Refund is available after one hour and is the known limit: it does not require the pages to still be dead.
